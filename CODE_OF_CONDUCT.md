# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CounterScene maintainers and contributors are committed to a harassment-free,
welcoming community regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive technical feedback.
- Focus discussions on the project and its users.
- Do not harass, threaten, insult, publish private information, or use sexualized
  language or imagery.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and participation that
violate these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately through the
maintainer contact channel listed on the repository profile. Reports will be
handled confidentially to the extent practical.
